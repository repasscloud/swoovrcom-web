# Corporate-site marketing review

Reviewed the full marketing pack: original brief; strategy; research/opportunities; execution playbook; developer requirements; draft reconciliation; consolidated reading edition; website identity; waitlist and independent-domain notes. Cross-checked the product brief, behaviour rules, design, trust/identity, venue/commerce, handoff and decision documents. The original ChatGPT source is provenance, not an approved capability list.

## Confirmed direction

- The corporate surface is swoovr.com. Application hosting remains independent.
- Broad adult intentions remain part of the story. Dating is a useful acquisition focus, not permission to erase friendship or consensual casual connections from the product brief.
- Required and excluded intentions, reciprocal visibility, profile substance and optional venue experiences are concrete concepts to explain.
- Website interest is not identity verification, email ownership confirmation, app admission or a paid entitlement.
- Adelaide is the initial planned beta focus. No launch date, local membership count, partner logo, contracted venue, testimonial, price or reward is invented.

## Implemented for local review

The homepage now leads with purpose and practical choices. Detailed trust information is on safety and verification pages; venue operators have a dedicated journey and preselected enquiry topic. Beta interest collects a short message, device and optional availability. Contact follows the helpful-context-plus-form pattern from the owner's RePass Cloud example, without copying its response-time promise or enterprise-service copy.

Major Australian cities and Other replace free text. Social links live in JSON; known links are used and unknown icons retain requested placeholders. Shared footer provides clear routes to company, product, trust, news and contact information.

Five draft stories explain purpose, intention boundaries, Adelaide-first development, verification and venues. No fictional milestone has been published. Production news provides useful links to the product story until an article is approved. Draft content is visible only in local Astro development and carries noindex metadata. Built output drives the sitemap, so future publication is automatic.

## Open operating items before public promotion

The website implementation does not finish the app's verification provider selection, moderation staffing, supported pilot scope, venue contracts or commercial terms. Those remain product/operating decisions. Newsletter campaigns still need tested email ownership, unsubscribe, suppression and send-time consent handling. Enquiries need an authorised person checking the database and processing requests; no notification delivery or response-time guarantee is implied.

Before an eventual main release, set Cloudflare’s deploy command to `npm run release` and add the build-only migration secret as documented. Set retention cleanup for both tables and verify production submissions privately. Recheck the three placeholder social URLs when the owner supplies them. No deployment or main push was performed.
