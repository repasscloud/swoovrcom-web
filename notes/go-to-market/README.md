## Evidence and decision labels

- **CONFIRMED:** directly observed in this checkout or an identified public source. Public-source verification confirms the published information, not acceptance of Swoovr or effectiveness of a tactic.
- **DEFAULT:** a recommended operating choice to review. It is not an approved company decision.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** a number, message, segment or tactic to test. None of the proposed acquisition or retention thresholds is an established industry benchmark.
- **OPEN:** account access, eligibility, implementation, price or commercial terms remain unverified.

The current source and live homepage show a functioning launch-interest form, illustrative portraits, privacy choices and the existing broad connection message. This strategy does not assume those systems already exist.

## Decisions proposed for review

1. Adelaide first; no national paid acquisition in the first 90 days.
2. Dating with clearer intentions as the initial acquisition message; validate friendship as a separate demand stream before activating it broadly.
3. Instagram plus permission-based local partnerships as the active acquisition channels; email as the activation channel.
4. City waitlist first, optional beta application second; referrals only after consent, ownership confirmation and attribution work.
5. Founder recognition for qualifying Adelaide beta participants, independent of subscription. A 30-day non-renewing Premium grant may be added when costs and paid features are understood.
6. No cash referral bounties, permanent paid entitlements, beauty/status selection, public queue or fake scarcity.
7. Public launch and city expansion depend on user-level compatible participation, retention and operational readiness.

**Planning resource default:** use scenario A as the provisional execution baseline. Actual founder hours and any cash commitment still need a company allocation; the alternative scenarios are options, not funding approvals. No tactic in this document authorises purchases or outreach.
