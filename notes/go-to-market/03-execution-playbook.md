Prepared 18 October 2026. [Pack index](README.md). Activities are proposed, not already authorised outreach or approved implementation. All cash amounts are AUD planning allowances, not quotes.

Late-December/January response patterns and volunteer/university availability may slow research. Do not extrapolate holiday participation to ordinary weeks.

Weekly allowances illustrate tasks, not additive monthly commitments. Use the monthly ceiling in [budget scenarios](01-strategy.md#i-budget-and-resources). Engineering and legal/advisory work require separate scoped estimates. No week requires a public release.

### Weekly operating rhythm

Monday: 30-minute local participation/support review and select one experiment. Tuesday: record/capture content and publish the principal item. Wednesday: partner/research conversations. Thursday: publish one practical explanation or development update. Friday: review confirmed registrations, acquisition costs, consent/suppression and support queue; decide what to stop. Content/community replies occur during published business-day coverage. Beta incident response needs separate explicit coverage, including outside ordinary marketing hours if members are active then.

Do not schedule more acquisition than the support team can handle. During a release or incident, reduce content and pause invitations.

A build can be technically ready while the local network is not. A waitlist can be large while moderation is not ready. Both product and operating gates must pass before broader admission.

## Email, community and lifecycle programme

### Consent and cadence

Separate a requested confirmation/security action from commercial launch promotion. A purely functional ownership-confirmation message should contain no upsell or referral push. Beta invitations, product previews, founder rewards and launch announcements should be treated as consented marketing by default; calling them “transactional” does not remove commercial-message requirements. Essential account/security messages after account creation need their own appropriate basis and must not become marketing workarounds.

ACMA requires consent, clear sender/contact information and a usable unsubscribe for commercial messages; honour unsubscribe within five working days and keep the facility working for at least 30 days. The recommended implementation suppresses immediately. [ACMA](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam).

Segment by confirmed city, communication consent, beta interest, supported device, invitation state, activation and last meaningful action. Do not use sensitive identity/intent preferences as email subjects or ad audiences. Keep person-level preferences inside restricted product/research systems. Use neutral notification previews and offer channel controls so a message does not unnecessarily reveal dating-app participation on a shared device.

| Trigger / segment | Timing and sample subject | Purpose / sample content | Type and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Track replies, confirmations, applications, acceptance and activation; open rates are unreliable and tracking itself requires privacy review. Start without open tracking. Cap routine pre-launch marketing at one monthly update plus genuinely relevant individual invitations, with a maximum of two marketing messages in seven days across automation paths. One welcome/confirmation sequence is not a licence to send overlapping campaigns. Suppress invitation reminders immediately after acceptance, decline, complaint or unsubscribe.

Existing list consent is for launch updates. Importing an old encrypted notification does not prove address ownership or authorise an expanded promotional programme. Apply the migration procedure in [developer requirements](04-developer-requirements.md), preserve original evidence/retention and never treat an unconfirmed legacy address as a verified subscriber.

### Community rules and moderation

An official Reddit community should state that it is run by Swoovr/RePass Cloud, whether the app is available, and what help it can provide. Pin product status, rules, FAQ and private support route. Prohibit doxxing, profile exposure, harassment, unsolicited sexual material, private-message recruitment, scam links and public person ratings. Do not accept safety reports in a public thread that exposes the subject or reporter; direct them to the private process and act on any immediate concern.

A founder can participate transparently in other communities if permitted. Moderators decide what is spam; there is no universal safe “10% promotion” rule. [Reddit spam guidance](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam). Do not recruit through competitor-app messages, harvest members, evade bans or create apparently independent supporters.

## Risks and deliberate exclusions

| Failure mode | Early signal | Practical mitigation / owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Thin local participation | Lower-tail users see few eligible active people | Pause broad recruitment, recruit reciprocal overlap, narrow the advertised offer transparently; product/research |
| 2. Demographic or social-circle imbalance | One source supplies most users; some groups poorly served | Multiple permission-based sources, optional inclusive research, privacy-safe coverage review; marketing |
| 3. Intentions fragment the network | Many profiles but few reciprocal choices | Phase modes, explain boundaries, recruit by demonstrated demand; product |
| 4. Fake/duplicate registrations | Confirmation failures, suspicious repeated patterns | Ownership confirmation, rate controls, dedupe, reward holds and appeals; engineering |
| 5. Referral farming | Bursts with no real applications/activity | Capped finite benefits, human review and no admission guarantees; operations |
| 6. Weak creator audience | High views, few confirmed local applicants | Small fixed deliverables, aggregate audience evidence and capped pilot; marketing |
| 7. Premature promotion | Expectations grow while product value absent | Publish current/planned claims register; keep spend small; founder |
| 8. Launch delay and fatigue | Replies ask “is this ever launching?”, old list unresponsive | Monthly honest updates, optional reconfirmation, respect retention deadline; lifecycle owner |
| 9. Deceptive exclusivity | Confusion/complaints over queue or places | Real capacity explanation, no fabricated counters, open application route; founder |
| 10. Privacy/outings | Sensitive subjects, leaked cohort or partner data | Neutral messages, restricted data access, suppression of small cells, no ad uploads; privacy owner |
| 11. Brand confusion | People cannot say whether this is dating/friendship or which domain to use | Clear first use case, swoovr.com campaigns, distinct app content later; brand/product |
| 12. Advertising/store rejection | Account warnings, delayed review | Preflight actual category, build differentiation and moderation, alternative organic routes; product/marketing |
| 13. Excessive acquisition cost | CPQR acceptable but activation poor, contribution insufficient | Matured-cohort economics, hard caps, stop weak sources; finance/marketing |
| 14. Poor activation | Delivered invites but incomplete profiles | Observe onboarding, reduce friction and unnecessary sensitive fields, fix device issues; product |
| 15. Safety incident | Serious reports, repeated offender, response gap | Staffed escalation, tested block/report, invitation pause and fair appeals; trust and safety |
| 16. Early expansion | Other-city signups rise while Adelaide coverage falls | Independent city gates, protect existing support and recruitment; leadership |
| 17. Unfinished features overclaimed | Marketing screens differ from available behaviour | Claims register tied to build evidence, prototype labels, correction process; product/marketing |
| 18. Founder liabilities | Confusion over badge vs paid entitlement | Plain terms, independent fields, capped grants, no lifetime discounts; product/finance |
| 19. Support burnout | Slow responses, founder development interrupted | Published coverage, daily load review, limited waves and funded help; operations |
| 20. Event no-shows or unsuitable environment | RSVPs but few attendees; accessibility complaints | Small preconfirmed event, reminders with consent, accessible/alcohol-free design, host brief; partnerships |
| 21. Analytics overstates performance | Platform conversions exceed unique confirmed people | Operational dedupe, consistent windows, consent-scoped denominators, no view-through guesswork; engineering/marketing |
| 22. Consent failure during migration | Old contacts added to broader campaign, opt-outs reappear | Original scope retained, confirmation and suppression reconciliation, import audit; privacy/lifecycle |
| 23. Free product is too restricted | New members cannot experience a useful connection | Test core free journey before pushing subscriptions; product/finance |
| 24. Good outcomes look like churn | People leave because they met someone | Optional respectful exit reasons; distinguish successful exits from dissatisfaction without forcing continued use; research |

Deliberately avoid purchased followers, paid-positive reviews, fictitious member counters, fake matches, misleading countdowns, cash-for-dates, competitions that obscure consent, attractiveness-based admission, indefinite lifetime paid access, gender-war content, underage-adjacent recruitment, sexualised ads, scraped outreach and public reputation scores for people. Avoid broad national PR bursts or a Product Hunt contest until local usefulness can survive the attention. Some of these tactics can create attention; they also create the wrong incentives or obligations.
