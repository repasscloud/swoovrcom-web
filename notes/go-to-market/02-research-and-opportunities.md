## Public relations and founder visibility

### Angles that could earn interest

1. **A useful local experiment:** what it takes to build a dating network in Adelaide, with real participant evidence and a candid explanation of why national signup totals mislead.
2. **A concrete product choice:** demonstrable visibility/intentions controls, tested with independent participants. Explain limits rather than announcing “the safest dating app.”
3. **Local community infrastructure:** a permitted venue collaboration that provides an actual experience, with host and participant permission to discuss it.
4. **Founder operating story:** a small Australian software company choosing bounded testing and measurable usefulness over manufactured traction. Relevant to business outlets after there is a specific decision/result to discuss.
5. **Local dating insight:** publish a properly consented survey with questions, sample size, recruitment method and limitations. A convenience sample is not representative of all Adelaide singles.

“We launched another dating app” is usually insufficient. Do not invent research findings, foreground someone's intimate story without specific consent, or turn a safety incident into promotional content.

### Referrals and subscription incentives: evidence limits

Current official pages describe [Tinder subscriptions](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004487406-Tinder-subscriptions), [Bumble paid plans](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/32668790872733-Understanding-Bumble-s-paid-features-and-subscription-plans) and [Feeld free/Majestic membership](https://support.feeld.co/hc/en-gb). These establish freemium/paid-feature packaging, not that a new Swoovr discount would convert profitably. This research did not verify a standing Australia-wide friend-referral reward or founder lifetime entitlement across the eight named dating competitors. That is an evidence gap, not a claim that no targeted or temporary offers exist. Likewise, Raya's application-only listing does not establish how referrals are weighted. Avoid copying screenshots of region/account-specific promotions as universal rules.

### Why these businesses can stall at launch

The commercial inference is that reciprocal demand is harder than ordinary lead generation. Each filter partitions available people; early non-response teaches people not to return; low retention then makes the next recruit's experience worse. Referrals can reinforce a single social circle, national press disperses participation, incentives attract people who want the prize, and early dating-app users may already have multiple competing apps. Product failures, weak moderation and a vague identity add friction at precisely the point the network needs coordinated participation.

Swoovr should borrow concentration, trusted introductions, clear intent and real-world utility. It should avoid borrowing large-company media spend, vanity launch rankings, undisclosed ambassadors, opaque status selection, permanent discounts or global reach as a substitute for local experience. No reliable public evidence established a universal best referral incentive or successful initial user count.

## Australian consent, privacy, safety and consumer requirements

- **Commercial email:** use clear permission, identify the sender, provide contact details and a simple unsubscribe. ACMA states requests must be honoured within five working days and the unsubscribe facility must remain functional at least 30 days. Build immediate suppression as the operational default. The Spam Act applies regardless of company size. [ACMA guidance](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam), [consent expectations](https://www.acma.gov.au/articles/2024-06/consent-expectations-businesses-using-direct-marketing).
- **Data minimisation:** sexual orientation/practices and some biometric information can be sensitive information. Treat beta preference collection as a distinct justified activity with explicit appropriate consent and access controls; do not collect it in advertising lead forms. Applicability of the Privacy Act to a small business depends on facts and exceptions; do not assume turnover provides a blanket exemption. [OAIC personal-information guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/your-privacy-rights/your-personal-information/what-is-personal-information), [small business guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/small-business), [APP 3](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-3-app-3-collection-of-solicited-personal-information).
- **Consumer claims:** ensure feature, scarcity, pricing and reward promises are accurate and substantiated. Explain total subscription price, billing period, renewal and cancellation before purchase; avoid free trials that disguise obligations. [ACCC misleading claims](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/advertising-and-promotions/false-or-misleading-claims), [June 2026 subscription-pricing enforcement example](https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/online-travel-agency-edreams-pays-penalties-and-gives-undertaking-over-allegedly-misleading-subscription-prices).
- **Safety operations:** use reporting, blocking, documented decisions and escalation before exposing strangers to one another. Australia's voluntary online dating code is an industry code; eSafety says it does not enforce that code. Swoovr has no verified signatory/compliance status and must not imply certification. Separately assess applicable online-safety duties for the actual app and features before beta. [eSafety online dating](https://www.esafety.gov.au/key-topics/staying-safe/online-dating), [Safety by Design](https://www.esafety.gov.au/industry/safety-by-design).
- **App stores:** user-generated content, moderation, privacy, account deletion and payment rules need a release review.
