Swoovr — Pre-Launch Marketing & Go-to-Market Strategy
Prepared for RePass Cloud Pty Ltd | 18 October 2026 | Initial market: Adelaide, South Australia
A. Executive go-to-market recommendation
Swoovr should launch as an Adelaide-first, invitation-led dating and social discovery platform, using a city-based waitlist, permanent founding-member recognition and controlled onboarding to build a concentrated community before public release.
The objective is not to make Swoovr famous before launch. It is to make Swoovr sufficiently desirable, trusted and locally populated that people joining it have a realistic opportunity to connect with compatible people.
The central commercial problem
Dating apps have a network-effect problem that conventional software products do not.
A productivity app can be useful with one customer. A dating app generally cannot.
An Adelaide user who downloads Swoovr, completes their profile and finds nobody compatible nearby may abandon the product regardless of its design, functionality or subscription price.
This leads to five strategic decisions.
1. Adelaide comes first. Build a locally relevant audience before advertising nationally.
2. Use invitations to control onboarding, not manufacture status. Limited access should represent genuine testing capacity and the need for compatible local users.
3. Treat founding membership as permanent recognition. Reward early supporters without creating permanent subscription liabilities.
4. Market intentions and control before features. Explain why people should care about Swoovr, not just what the application can do.
5. Measure successful connections, not registrations. Public launch should depend on evidence of local compatibility, conversations, retention and safety.
What Swoovr should aim to become known for
My proposed positioning is:
Swoovr — Connect on your terms.
Dating, relationships, friendship and meaningful connections, without everyone having to want the same thing.

This gives the brand room to accommodate different adult relationship goals without presenting itself exclusively as a hookup application.

Is Adelaide the right first market?
Yes, as a controlled commercial experiment—not because Adelaide guarantees success.
The advantages are practical: Swoovr can concentrate recruitment, conduct local interviews, build community relationships and potentially organise events without supporting a national rollout.
The disadvantage is that a smaller market gives each compatibility segment a smaller potential audience. A user seeking a long-term heterosexual relationship and a user seeking a same-sex casual connection may have access to completely different effective networks despite living in the same city.
Therefore, Adelaide should be treated as several overlapping matching markets, not one population.
I would initially concentrate recruitment around central Adelaide and nearby suburbs, then expand coverage where actual demand supports it. The application should remain inclusive; marketing recruitment should be deliberately balanced rather than artificially restricting who can participate.
What I would not do
I would not purchase a large national advertising campaign, create fake member profiles, sell lifetime Premium subscriptions, promise an arbitrary launch date or open registrations nationally just because the website generates interest.
I would also avoid portraying Swoovr as an affair or adult-encounter service in paid campaigns. Google's current dating-advertising policy distinguishes general dating from services explicitly associated with hookups, affairs and other sexual encounters, with additional restrictions and certification requirements. This is a commercial reason to keep advertising aligned with Swoovr's broader connection-focused positioning—not to misrepresent what users can actually seek within the product.

D. Invitation, exclusivity and founding-member strategy
Recommended model: The Adelaide First programme
There should be three distinct groups, each with different rights.
Group	How they qualify	What they receive
Waitlist member	Registers interest and verifies email	Development updates and potential future invitation
Private beta participant	Selected from an eligible, relevant local cohort	Temporary access to unfinished software and feedback opportunities
Founding member	Meets published eligibility criteria and activates during the founding programme	Permanent gold Swoovr founding badge and recognition
A waitlist registration should not automatically grant founding status, and a referral should not automatically grant access.

The number 500 should only be published after evaluating likely demand and the programme's value. If the number is advertised, the actual cap, eligibility window and remaining allocations must be truthful and auditable.
Permanent recognition should not imply lifetime free Premium.
Recommended eligibility: an eligible Adelaide-area adult who completes genuine account activation within the published founding period, accepts the programme terms and passes applicable anti-abuse checks.
The allocation must not be consumed merely by unverified email addresses or reservation bots.
For account deletion and re-registration, keep founder restoration separate from subscriptions and personal profile data. Use the previously proposed privacy-preserving recovery-token model, with verified email control and server-side cryptographic verification. Recovery must not automatically recreate a deleted account's sensitive content.
Invitation and referral design
Rule	Marketing decision	Engineering implementation
Invite format	Short, shareable, unguessable code	12-character Crockford Base32 random code, with collision checking
Redemption	Single use by default	Atomic redemption transaction
Validity	30 days once issued	Explicit UTC expiry; configurable by campaign
Geographic scope	Adelaide-first waves	Eligibility by declared city, with optional independently verified region where necessary
Attribution	First valid referring user	Immutable accepted attribution plus fraud investigation override
Referral success	Requires genuine activation	Count only after email verification, onboarding and anti-abuse checks
Rewards	Recognition or limited trial	Separate entitlements ledger

Revocation	Revoke unused codes and fraudulent rewards	Audited admin actions and reason codes
Personal data	Do not disclose invitees to referrers	Aggregate counts, not identity or sensitive preferences
The human-readable invite code is not a security credential for identity verification. It should be server-generated using a cryptographically secure random source, stored as a keyed hash or other appropriate protected representation, and rate-limited at redemption.

Referral rewards
Do not pay for email registrations. Reward qualified participation.
Milestone	Proposed reward	Commercial control
1 activated referral	Referrer recognition	No cash cost
3 activated referrals	Priority consideration for next appropriate access wave	Not a guaranteed invitation
5 activated referrals	Optional 14-day Plus trial	One-time, non-transferable; subject to commercial testing

All referrals must be voluntary. A person's choice to use a dating application should not be exposed to other members through the referral dashboard.
Critical design choice: a high-referral participant should not jump ahead of someone whose inclusion would improve mutual compatibility in the next onboarding cohort.

Milestone gates after the first 90 days

The first 90 days should produce a measured audience and a launch system, not force an unfinished application into beta.
