## A. Executive go-to-market recommendation

Swoovr should build a useful Adelaide network through a city-qualified waitlist, trusted local recruitment and supported onboarding waves. Permanent founder recognition can reward contribution without creating permanent paid-feature liabilities. Public release should follow evidence of mutually relevant participation, workable safety/support and retention.

The objective is to make Swoovr credible and locally useful while it is being developed, then convert interest into real participation. An unfamiliar dating app that feels empty after someone completes onboarding can lose that person's trust regardless of its branding or subscription price. A thousand scattered registrations can be less valuable than a smaller group with reciprocal preferences who can actually meet nearby.

### The five central decisions

1. **Adelaide first:** concentrate learning, recruitment and support before national acquisition.
2. **Capacity-based invitations:** admit supported groups without manufacturing status, fake queues or deceptive scarcity.
3. **Recognition independent of payment:** a founder badge is not lifetime Premium or an identity/safety endorsement.
4. **Intentions and control as the proposed value:** explain the problem before listing features, and mark unfinished capabilities as planned.
5. **Participation before scale:** measure relevant active options, reciprocal conversations, useful outcomes and operating load rather than signup totals alone.

### Positioning and the initial audience

Keep the current “Your kind of connection” brand line. Test the imported alternative **“Connect on your terms”** with the same explicit category/location explanation:

> Swoovr is a dating and social discovery app in development for adults 18+. We’re building around clearer intentions and more control over who can discover and contact you. Our first planned beta will focus on Adelaide.

The slogans alone are too broad to explain why someone should try a new dating service. “On your terms” must not imply implemented controls, unlimited choice or guaranteed results.

**DEFAULT:** begin research/message testing with Adelaide adults aged 25–39 who can comfortably meet around the CBD/inner suburbs. Include adjacent adult ages and different identities, cultures, accessibility needs and intentions in research. This is a focus for learning, not an exclusion or a proven commercial segment. Let reciprocal demand and actual product readiness determine the first beta group.

Keep the inclusive brand, but phase functionality where necessary. Dating, friendship and casual connections create overlapping networks with different permissions and expectations. Do not imply that every mode is ready or that every preference has enough nearby participants. Friendship interest is not consent to romantic contact. Casual intentions can be legitimate and explicit without turning the whole brand into sexualised advertising.

Sensitive preferences need particular care. OAIC guidance identifies sexual orientation as sensitive information and addresses consent for sensitive-information direct marketing where applicable. Assess Swoovr's exact obligations and never assume a marketing opt-in permits uploading intimate preferences to ad platforms. [OAIC guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/direct-marketing).

### Is Adelaide appropriate?

Yes, conditionally: local presence can make interviews, relationships, testing and support practical. The smaller market also means each reciprocal age/location/identity/intent intersection can be thin. Treat Adelaide as overlapping matching markets, rather than one undifferentiated user pool. Validate the proposition and access routes before committing substantial spend. A larger city is not an automatic remedy for a weak product promise or inaccessible audience.

Avoid national paid campaigns, fake profiles, lifetime paid access, arbitrary release promises, public person ratings, beauty/status selection and misleading member counters. Do not mislabel the service as friendship-only to evade dating-ad rules.

Recommended sequence: **credible website and city interest → consented research and qualified candidates → supported private beta → bounded public Adelaide launch → retention-led city expansion.**

## D. Invitation, exclusivity and founding-member strategy

### Three different rights

| Group | Eligibility | Right or benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed city-list subscriber | Confirmed email, stated city/adult declaration and valid communication consent | Relevant updates; no app profile or admission guarantee |
| Beta participant | Current eligible application, supported device/location and invitation into a supported wave | Access to a specific unfinished build with stated limitations and feedback/support routes |
| Adelaide founding member | Proposed beta activation in the published period plus one accessible feedback/testing contribution | Optional permanent recognition while the account exists, independent of payment |

A signup is not a badge, a referral is not access, and a badge is not verification. Publish rules before enrolment. Keep an application route open to adults without personal referrals.

### Founder recognition

Use a gold bird badge visibly different from any verification badge. Subscription cancellation and ordinary inactivity do not remove it. An optional 30-day non-renewing Premium grant may be considered once real benefits and costs exist; no payment card or automatic subscription. Lifetime Premium/discounts are not recommended.

The imported draft proposed 500 founder places. Treat that as an unvalidated hypothesis, not a default scarcity device. Prefer a defined beta eligibility window and real capacity-controlled waves. If a numerical cap is later justified, publish and audit it accurately. Inviting founders to exclusive activities must not confer safety/moderation priority or imply every founder has a guaranteed event ticket.

Founder revocation is limited to error/fraud or applicable account termination, with reasons and appeal. Critical feedback, unsubscribe and nonpayment cannot cause revocation.

### Founder recognition after deletion

The imported text refers to a previously proposed recovery-token model, but the supplied brief does not establish one. **DEFAULT:** no automatic post-deletion recovery promise. **OPEN:** assess a voluntary recognition-only claim mechanism separately before offering it.

A cryptographic receipt might demonstrate an award, but it does not inherently prove the holder is the original person or keep its contents confidential. Do not retain a hidden copy of deleted profiles, treat an email/token alone as identity proof, or let a recognition claim bypass current safety eligibility. Key rotation, stolen/transferred tokens, duplicate claims, data retention, appeals and service retirement all need a design. [Developer recovery requirements](go-to-market/04-developer-requirements.md#founder-recognition-after-account-deletion).

### Separate sharing from access

| Rule | Consolidated default |
| --- | --- |
| Public referral link | Opaque random ID, proposed minimum 96-bit entropy; attributes voluntary confirmed interest, does not unlock the app |
| Private beta invitation | Separate email-bound one-use secret, proposed minimum 128-bit entropy, hashed/protected at rest |
| Invitation validity | Seven days plus fair extension/reissue; a 30-day campaign is a possible explicit later test, not a conflicting default |
| Redemption | Atomic, idempotent, recipient- and wave-bound; never bypasses ordinary app eligibility |
| Attribution | First successful signup attribution, confirmed by the person, then locked subject to audited corrections |
| Initial referral recognition | At three confirmed local referrals, private contribution recognition and optional feedback-session place; no admission guarantee |
| Later finite reward | Only after cost/abuse testing: activated eligible referrals may earn a limited non-renewing grant, capped at 14 referral-derived days/person |
| Fraud control | Conservative dedupe, destination/network throttling, reward hold, human review and appeal; shared IP alone is not proof of duplication |
| Privacy | No contact imports or unsolicited Swoovr invitations; referrer sees aggregate credit, not names/preferences/activity |
| Geography | Stated city, approximate area and practical local availability; no exact GPS/address required to join a list |
| Revocation | Auditable errors/abuse process; no public shaming or fabricated urgency |

Twelve Base32 characters encode at most 60 random bits; the imported example is not retained as the private-access-token design. Invite codes are not identity credentials. Keep tokens out of logs/analytics/referrers and prevent automatic link-preview GET requests from consuming access.

Start an external supported beta wave around 30–50 people only if product/safety/support are ready. This tests onboarding, not public-launch liquidity. Add complementary local participation based on evidence; only pilot 100–150 concurrent invitations when support can handle it. Synthetic/staff accounts stay in segregated testing and never inflate actual membership or compatibility metrics.

At the low end of B/C, reduce activity before exceeding the ceiling. These allocations do not buy a full marketing team. Keep actual spending contingent on company allocation and verified quotes.

Use `qualified beta candidate = confirmed email + Adelaide interest + adult declaration + supported device + current willingness to test`. CPQR is campaign spend divided by unique qualified candidates. It is not activated-user or paying-customer CAC.

A hypothetical A$300 campaign at A$1.50 CPC yields 200 visits. At 2%, 5% or 10% visitor-to-qualified-candidate conversion, it yields 4, 10 or 20 candidates at A$75, A$30 or A$15 CPQR. These are sensitivity inputs, not market benchmarks. At 40% activation, activated-user acquisition cost is 2.5 times CPQR. Compare it with conservative contribution after fees, service/support, discounts and refunds before scaling.

Google requires appropriate dating certification. TikTok's current policy requires eligible dating advertisers to work with sales and restrict to adults; Meta/Reddit account-specific category requirements remain checks before spend. Organic account access is not ad approval. [Google policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15328393?hl=en), [TikTok policy](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-adult-content), [full policy review](go-to-market/02-research-and-opportunities.md#advertising-policy-checks).

Stop a test for consent/measurement failure, misleading creative, policy rejection or safety overload. Give every experiment a spend cap and defined metric. Pause at an initial A$150 checkpoint if no qualified candidates; diagnose a specific problem before buying another attempt. No automatic spending to the ceiling.

## J. Launch-readiness dashboard

Track the funnel from local interest to sustained useful participation, with consistent windows and denominators.

| Stage | Metric / decision use |
| --- | --- |
| Attention | Relevant local reach and consent-observed website visitor estimates; followers/impressions are diagnostic only |
| Interest | Unique confirmed city subscribers, source/unknown classification, contactable current list and opt-outs |
| Qualification | Supported device/current willingness; email confirmation is not age/identity/residence verification |
| Invitations | Issued, delivered, bounced, revoked, redeemed, expired and declined; acceptance = redeemed/delivered |
| Activation | Usable eligible profile and deliberate discovery readiness within seven days; activation = activated/redeemed, plus activated/delivered full-funnel rate |
| Participation | Per-person mutually eligible, discoverable options active in prior seven days; median, lower percentiles and coverage |
| Interaction | First seven-day two-way conversations and optional private usefulness feedback, with response rate |
| Outcomes | Optional consented meeting/successful-exit feedback; no forced location or intimate details |
| Retention | D7/D28 on fixed matured cohorts/windows; distinguish dissatisfaction from successful exits |
| Economics | CPQR, activated-user CAC, paying CAC, paid conversion, refunds/cancellation and contribution scope |
| Safety/support | Severity, acknowledgement/action times, repeat issues and backlog; more reports can reflect better reporting |

An active person performs an intentional discovery action or sends a message; a background event is insufficient. Compatible options must satisfy reciprocal location/age/identity/intent/mode permissions and exclude blocks, hidden/suspended accounts and test profiles. Show privacy-protected distributions rather than crude gender ratios.

**Initial experimental gates:** at least 40% invitation acceptance, 60% activation among redeemed invites, 80% of active people with at least ten eligible active options, 30% with an early two-way conversation, D7 retention 30% and D28 20%. These are hypotheses for review, not proven standards or sufficient conditions on their own. Inspect lower-tail experience, qualitative usefulness, uncertainty and underserved groups across multiple supported waves. The detailed plan proposes four weekly participation snapshots, matured cohorts and minimum directional sample sizes; small samples cannot establish readiness.

Public Adelaide release also needs working moderation/report/block, real support coverage, app/store readiness and accurate commercial/privacy terms. If a material audience cannot find useful participation, retain beta or honestly bound the offer. Review expansion only after stable Adelaide operations, repeatable acquisition and a separately qualified second-city network; registrations elsewhere are insufficient.

## K. Technical requirements for developers

The current [OpenAPI contract](../api/waitlist.openapi.json) documents notification submission, not a CRM. Reuse/evolve it deliberately when implementation is approved; do not reinterpret the current `consent` field or 202 response silently.

| Requirement | Necessary capability and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Waitlist / city | Durable pending/confirmed/suppressed states, city correction, conservative dedupe, non-enumerating public responses |
| Consent / retention | Versioned purpose/channel evidence, independent analytics, immediate suppression, simple unsubscribe and deletion/expiry handling |
| Legacy records | Preserve original consent/expiry, import as unconfirmed, reconcile previous opt-outs, secure decryption/exports |
| Beta applications | Private, purpose-limited optional information; supported-device/readiness and transparent eligibility |
| Invitations | Separate recipient-bound access secrets, atomic redemption, expiry/reissue/revocation, wave caps and pause |
| Referrals | Distinct attribution IDs, voluntary signup, provisional rewards, caps, fraud review and no disclosure of referred identities |
| Founder status | Independent award, optional display, terms/evidence, fair revocation and subscription independence |
| Founder recovery | OPEN separate design; no restored sensitive data, hidden indefinite retention, transfer guarantee or sanctions bypass |
| Finite entitlements | Explicit redemption/start/end, no card/renewal by default, idempotent bounded grants and store-rule review |
| Analytics / reporting | Deduplicated state transitions, test-account exclusion, known/unknown source, no sensitive third-party events |
| Administration | Least-privilege access, strong authentication, audited bulk actions/exports and small-cell suppression |
| Operational readiness | Real delivery/suppression checks, report/block/escalation drills and support pause; mocked success is not inbox/integration proof |

Keep tokens out of URLs/logs/referrers where avoidable; prevent scanners from redeeming through GET. A hash, opaque ID or signed receipt is not automatically anonymous or confidential. No new storage/mail/identity/verification provider is selected by this document.
