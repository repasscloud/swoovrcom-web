“We should meet sometime” is easy to say. Choosing where, agreeing on a plan and making it comfortable for both people can take more thought.

Swoovr’s venue concept is about that step between conversation and meeting: optional experiences with enough information to make a decision together.

## A useful option, not an obligation

A café, an activity space or a relaxed place to talk could offer a simple starting point. People should also remain free to make their own arrangements. Buying an experience shouldn’t be compulsory for making a connection.

We’re exploring Adelaide venue conversations as part of the wider product design. This is a partnership concept, not an announcement of a live booking catalogue or contracted venue network.

## The details make the experience

A useful offer needs clear inclusions, availability and conditions. People need to understand the price, what happens if plans change and how a booking or voucher is honoured.

Venue staff need a practical redemption process. They shouldn’t need access to private conversations or dating preferences to welcome guests.

Accessibility, transport options and quieter times can be just as useful as an attractive photograph of a space. We want to hear what venues know about helping people feel comfortable.

## A shared plan still needs individual choice

A venue listing doesn’t endorse someone’s behaviour or guarantee a safe meeting. Each person should be free to change their mind, choose their own transport and decide how much they share.

The goal is to support a plan people want, with clear expectations for the venue as well as the guests.

If you run an Adelaide venue, we’d like to understand your setting and the experience you could offer. Commercial terms, responsibilities and operating arrangements need discussion before anything goes live.
